Only 9% of open jobs are fully remote. Here is where they are

October 23, 2026 · Updated October 24, 2026 · 3 min read

Remote work is everywhere on social media, but how many job postings really offer it? This index collects open positions every day from public company career boards, so we can count instead of guess.

On October 24, 2026 the index held 268,835 open positions from 11,041 companies in 102 countries. This is how they split by workplace:

Fewer than one job in ten is fully remote. Hybrid, a few days a week at the office, is more common than remote.

Two things stand out:

  • Europe is the hybrid continent. Almost a third of European jobs are hybrid, more than twice the share in North America.
  • North America and Europe have about the same remote share, a little over one job in eight.

Why Brazil looks so different

Brazil has the most open jobs in the index, and only 1.6% of them are remote. Part of that is real: retail, pharmacies, hospitals and restaurants hire a lot, and those jobs are onsite by nature. Part of it is coverage: we read Brazil's big hiring platforms, which carry many store and operations roles, while for most other countries we read the career boards of tech and corporate employers.

So the number to keep in mind for Brazil is not the share but the count: 1,507 remote jobs open on a single day. If you are looking for remote work from Brazil, also look at international companies that hire across Latin America.

What this means if you are job hunting

  1. Do not filter on remote only. Hybrid roles are almost 1.5 times more common, and many ask for only one or two office days a week.
  2. Remote competition is global. A remote US posting gets applicants from many countries, so a strong, tailored CV matters more there.
  3. Check who may apply. Many "remote" jobs are remote inside one country only. The index reads the posting text to show where applicants must live.

How we counted

Each job is counted once, even when it appears on several sites. Workplace comes from the posting itself (its location field and text); 2.6% of postings do not say. The numbers are a snapshot of jobs open on October 24, 2026 and change every day.
